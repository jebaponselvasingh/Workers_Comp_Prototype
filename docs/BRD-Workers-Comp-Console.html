Section 01

Introduction

Product summary

LINEWORKER is a workers'-compensation claims console purpose-built for the US manufacturing sector. It presents an insurance-adjuster workflow enriched with an agentic AI copilot that provides claim-specific guidance (labor law, reserve adequacy, fraud checks, next-best-actions, similar-case outcomes) and generates stakeholder communications (emails, RTW letters). The system is organized around three roles, each with its own workspace.

Business objectives

  1. Accelerate the claim lifecycle against explicit SLAs — pick a claim in < 1 day, approve in < 5 days, settle in < 30 days, and achieve > 80% successful return-to-work (RTW).
  2. Prioritize adjuster attention on the highest-risk claims (litigation, fraud/SIU, RTW-blocked, surgery, pending approvals) via a computed priority score.
  3. Embed compliance & correctness — OSHA recordability, ICD-10 coding, state statutory benefit min/max, reserve adequacy checks.
  4. Give supervisors portfolio oversight — SLA performance, handler productivity/complexity, financial exposure, fraud/litigation concentration.
  5. Give analysts drill-down into fraud, trends, and KPIs.
  6. Reduce administrative friction — one-click templated emails, meeting scheduling, RTW-letter generation, and a domain glossary.

Scope

Covers all three roles and every interactive element: the login/role selector, global top bar, glossary, the handler workspace (queue + case detail + copilot + diary/meetings/emails + modals), and the supervisor/analyst portfolio dashboard.

Business rules on scoping

BR-ROLE-1Visible caseload MUST be limited to the claims mapped to that persona (employer-based partition). Supervisor "David Bline" and Analyst see the full portfolio; other supervisors see only their assigned employer groups.

3.3 Data model (shared foundation)

Every claim carries a 63-field schema. Key groups the UI depends on:

  • Identity / context — employeeId, claimId, policyNum, name, employer, sector, plant, state, region, role, dept, handler, supervisor.
  • Injury / clinical — injuryType, cause, bodyPart, bodyKey, icd, icdDesc, severity, sevScore, disability, recovery, surgeryRequired, treatmentPlan, prognosis, contraindications.
  • Lifecycle / status — stage (intake · investigation · treatment · settled), status, commStatus, returnStatus, doi, froiDate, assignDate, approvalDate, daysOpen, settlementDays.
  • Risk / compliance flags — risk, oshaRecordable, litigationFlag, attorneyRep, fraudScore, fraudFlag, plus derived siuReview, rtwBlocked, paymentDue.
  • Financials — aww, reserve, paidIndemnity, paidMedical, paidExpense, totalPaid, totalIncurred, and SLA fields.
  • AI enrichment — cpNextActions, cpSimilarCase, cpReserveNote, cpFraudIndicators.
  • Evidence — timeline, documents, photos.

Data profile of the seed set (100 claims): fraud flags = 13 · litigation = 3 · OSHA recordable = 64 · surgery required = 39 · 6 handlers · 3 supervisors · 10 employers · 17 states · 20 distinct injury types.

3.4 Glossary overlay

Slide-in panel with a live search box filtering 24 domain terms (FNOL, TTD, PPD, PTD, MMI, IME, AWW, RTW, CTS, HAVS, NIHL, OSHA 300, FROI, ICD-10, Reserve, Subro, PPE, EMG, ORIF, Arc Flash, SLA, LTD, WPI, Apportionment, VR).

FR-GLOS-1Search MUST filter by abbreviation, term, or definition text and show a "no matches" state.

3.5 Global limitations (all roles)

NFR-1No persistence — all edits, chats, notes, meetings, and emails are in-memory only; reload/logout discards everything. Production requires a persistent backend.
NFR-2Offline AI — the copilot calls the Anthropic API with no credentials, so it always falls back to deterministic canned answers from each claim's cp* fields. Production requires a secured, server-brokered AI integration.
NFR-3Native dialogs — several confirmations use alert(). Production should use in-app non-blocking notifications.
NFR-4Static content — external form links point to NY WCB / FNSB PDFs; benefit figures are illustrative and must be validated against live state schedules.

Stage grouping & priority

  • Filtered claims group into 4 collapsible sections with counts: 📥 Intake · 🔍 Investigation · 🩺 Treatment · ✅ Settled.
  • Within each stage, cards sort by a computed priority score: litigation +40, SIU +35, RTW-blocked +30, pending approval +25, payment due +20, surgery +15, plus 0.3×severity + 0.2×days-open, minus 100 if settled. Top-3 cards (score > 30) get a 🔺 marker.
  • Each card shows: priority marker, claim ID, days-open, a colored risk dot, worker name, badges FRAUD LITIG PAY DUE SIU, injury type, stage pill, employer.

FR-Q-1The filter dropdown MUST re-filter and re-render grouped, priority-sorted results.
FR-Q-2Cards MUST surface risk and the four operational flags at a glance.
FR-Q-3Selecting a card MUST drive the center and right panes to that claim.
FR-Q-4Priority scoring MUST elevate litigation, SIU, RTW-blocked, pending-approval, payment-due, and surgical claims.

4.2 Case Detail panel

Case header — worker name; meta line (claim ID · role · employer · state); injury summary (type — body part, cause · ICD-10 · severity); a badge row (stage pill + conditional Fraud Score / Litigation / Surgery / OSHA); and a risk gauge colored Low / Medium / High.

Tab bar — 6 tabs: Overview · Injury Diagram · Bills & Payments · Documents & ID · Photos (n) · AI Insights.

Tab 1 — Overview (stage-adaptive)

Always opens with a 4-step stage stepper (Intake → Investigation → Treatment → Settled). Body varies by stage:

  • Intake — intake summary + reported injury + document checklist (FROI, MEDAUTH, WAGE, INCIDENT, each Received/Missing) + actions + timeline.
  • Investigation — editable injury card (type, cause, body part, ICD-10, disability, recovery are inline-editable) + financials/reserve + benefit card + actions + full timeline.
  • Treatment — treatment-phase banner (Early / Active / Approaching-MMI) + bills-vs-reserve card (with jump to Bills) + Care & RTW coordination status (On Track / Coordination Gap / Awaiting Information / Legal) + benefit + actions + recent timeline.
  • Settled — settled banner + final payout breakdown + claim outcome + summary of actions taken.

Tab 1a — Benefit calculation & reserve

  • Editable comp-rate input (% of AWW; default 66.67%, or 100% for PTD when permanent + severity ≥ 85), with a ↺ reset control.
  • Shows weekly indemnity (clamped to state statutory min/max), indemnity type (TTD / TPD / PPD / PTD), and an auto-generated reserve-rationale paragraph.

Tab 2 — Injury Diagram

  • Interactive body silhouette with animated markers colored by severity; the primary injury pulses.
  • Editable body-part selector, an "+ Add another injury" popover (body part + type + severity → secondary marker; removable), and an editable severity score (0–100) that recomputes severity band + risk.

Tab 3 — Bills & Payments

  • Financial summary (Total Claim Amount, Paid To Date, Reserve Remaining, Reserve Check = Light / Adequate / Heavy).
  • Metrics: Weekly Indemnity, Installments Paid, Next Payment Due, Bills On File.
  • Week-by-week indemnity schedule (Paid / Due This Week / Upcoming / Pending Approval) and a medical bills list.

Tab 4 — Documents & ID

  • Path-based required forms — Path A minor / Path B follow-up / Path C fatality, with statutory forms (C-2F, C-3, RFA-1W, C-4.3, AFF-1…), timing, and ⬇ download links.
  • Visual employee ID card, and a claim documents list — clicking a row opens a document-viewer modal (FROI shows full injury detail).
Prototype limitation

The path field is not present in the data, so this always renders Path B. Production must classify each claim's path.

Tab 5 — Photos

Grid of incident/site photo cards (caption + source). Clicking a card opens a photo-viewer modal. Empty state supported.

Tab 6 — AI Insights

Similar case outcomes · Reserve adequacy review · Next best actions · Fraud risk indicators (score + red-flag list, or a low-risk confirmation) — all sourced from the claim's cp* fields.

FR-DET-1The Overview MUST adapt content to the claim's lifecycle stage.
FR-DET-2Inline edits (injury fields, body part, severity, comp rate, added injuries) MUST update the record and re-render dependent calculations.
FR-DET-3Reserve Check MUST compare projected remaining exposure (unpaid indemnity + unpaid medical) to the current reserve and classify Light / Adequate / Heavy.
FR-DET-4Documents and photos MUST open read-only viewer modals.

4.3 AI Adjuster Copilot panel

Two tabs: ⚡ Actions and 📓 Diary.

Actions tab

  • 7 quick-action buttons: Labor law & state rules · Similar case outcomes · Review RTW Policy · Reserve review · Fraud risk check · Next best actions · Data alignment note. All submit a claim-specific prompt except Review RTW Policy, which opens the RTW template modal.
  • Chat log with a seeded case-summary greeting, a free-text input, and a disclaimer.
FR-CP-1Quick actions MUST send claim-contextual prompts; the assistant MUST answer per claim (live model when available, otherwise the deterministic offline briefing).
FR-CP-2Chat history MUST be maintained per claim within the session.

Diary tab — 3 sub-tabs

  • 📓 Notes — greeting, today's date, active claim, today's meetings summary, and a reverse-chronological note list; add-note input tied to the current claim.
  • 📅 Meetings — date-sorted list with Upcoming/Done styling; actions ✓ Done / ✉ Email participants / Delete; two demo meetings seeded on first login; "＋ Schedule Meeting" opens the modal.
  • ✉ Emails — reverse-chronological sent list; "＋ Compose Email to Stakeholders" opens the composer.
FR-DIARY-1Handlers MUST log dated notes tied to a claim.
FR-DIARY-2Schedule / complete / delete / convert-to-email meetings.
FR-DIARY-3Compose, template, and log stakeholder emails.

4.4 Handler modals

  • Meeting Scheduler — 10 meeting types (3-Point Contact, RTW Conference, NCM Care Coordination, IME Preparation, Settlement Discussion, Physician Consultation, Employer Accommodation Review, Litigation Prep, Claim Review — Supervisor, Other), linked claim, date/time, participant checkboxes, notes, location.

  • RTW Policy Template — an editable RTW offer letter pre-populated from claim data, with ✏ Edit / 🖨 Print / 📋 Copy.
  • Document / Photo / generic viewer — read-only detail sheets.

Section 06
📊 Role 3 · Analytics

Data Analyst

As-built

The Analyst logs in over the full 100-claim portfolio and is shown the same dashboard as the Supervisor. Only the role badge and label differ, so everything in Section 05 is available to the Analyst today.

Intended purpose (per the tagline "Fraud, trends, KPI drill-down"): go deeper than the Supervisor's oversight — into fraud analytics, temporal/segment trends, and KPI decomposition.

6.1 Analyst requirements (target state)

FR-AN-1Fraud analytics — score distribution, the 13 flagged claims (≥ 55), cpFraudIndicators aggregation, SIU-review pipeline, and fraud rate by injury type / employer / handler.
FR-AN-2Trends — time-series on FNOL/DOI volume, average days-open, settlement cycle time, RTW rate, and cost; cohort comparisons (severity band, disability type, sector).
FR-AN-3KPI drill-down — every KPI, chart segment, and table row MUST be clickable to drill into the constituent claims (the capability the current read-only dashboard lacks).
FR-AN-4Segmentation — slice/filter by employer, sector, state/region, injury type, ICD-10, severity band, age group, gender, disability type.
FR-AN-5Financial analytics — paid vs. reserve vs. incurred; reserve-adequacy distribution across the portfolio; cost-driver analysis (surgery, litigation).
FR-AN-6Export — export filtered datasets and chart data for offline analysis / audit.
Gap

None of FR-AN-1…FR-AN-6 are differentiated from the Supervisor view today. Closing this gap is the core Analyst requirement.

Section 07

Cross-Cutting Requirements

7.1 SLA definitions

SLADefinitionTarget
PickFROI → handler assignment< 1 day
ApproveFROI → claim approval< 5 days
SettleFROI → settlement< 30 days
RTW Rate% of settled claims with successful RTW> 80%

7.2 Derived operational flags

  • SIU review — fraudFlag AND fraudScore ≥ 60.
  • RTW blocked — in treatment, under treatment, and (deterministic bucket OR high risk).
  • Payment due — in treatment and (deterministic bucket).

7.3 Compliance & correctness

OSHA recordability tracked and surfaced; ICD-10 coding on every claim; state statutory benefit min/max enforced in indemnity computation; path-based statutory forms (A/B/C) with timing guidance.

7.4 Limitations → production requirements

  1. Authentication & authorization — replace self-select roles/personas and the static HANDLER_MAP with real identity and an authoritative assignment source.
  2. Persistence — all case edits, notes, meetings, emails, and chats must persist to an auditable backend.
  3. AI integration — broker AI calls server-side with credentials, guardrails, and cited legal sources; the current client-side call is credential-less and always falls back offline.
  4. Analyst differentiation — build the fraud/trends/drill-down analyst workspace (§6).
  5. Drill-through — make supervisor/analyst KPIs, charts, and rows navigable into claims.
  6. Claim-path classification — populate the path field so Documents shows the correct A/B/C forms.
  7. SLA strip parity — recompute the top-bar SLA strip for all roles, not just handlers.
  8. Real integrations — email send, calendar scheduling, document management, print/export, live state benefit schedules.
  9. UX polish — replace alert() with in-app notifications; add empty/error/loading states throughout.
